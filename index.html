Will it give you the munchies?

Scan the QR code on a cannabis product’s lab report. Munchie Bot reads the results and estimates, from 1 to 10, how likely that product is to make you hungry, and shows you why.

For iPhone · Adults 21+

How it works

Lab reports list every cannabinoid and terpene in a batch. Munchie Bot turns that table into one number you can read at a glance.

  1. Scan the lab report

    Point your camera at the QR code on the label or certificate of analysis (COA). You can also paste a link or open a PDF.

  2. See the score and why

    A 1–10 estimate with a likely range and a confidence level, plus the THC, CBD and terpenes that pushed it up or down.

  3. Tune it to you

    Turn traits up or down in My sensitivities, and add your own rating to compare the estimate with your experience.

A quick look

Private by design

Munchie Bot has no accounts and no server that collects your data. What you scan and how you rate it stays on your iPhone.

Read the privacy policy

  • No sign-up or account
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • Scans and ratings stored only on your phone
  • Back up to Files or iCloud Drive whenever you like

Munchie Bot is an information tool for adults 21 and older where cannabis is legal. Scores are estimates based on lab data and published research, not medical advice. It doesn’t sell, deliver or advertise any products.
